# 2022 Nuclear Posture Review Public Excerpt

Nuclear weapons serve a narrow but consequential role in deterring strategic attacks, assuring allies, and preventing coercion in extreme circumstances. Public summaries emphasize that any employment would involve grave consequences and high-level political control.

## Declaratory Restraint

Public doctrine stresses that nuclear use would be contemplated only in extreme circumstances to defend vital interests. The text also highlights arms control, risk reduction, crisis communication, and the importance of maintaining credible non-nuclear options.
