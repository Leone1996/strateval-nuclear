# Dual-Use Ambiguity and Nuclear-Conventional Entanglement Excerpt

Public research on dual-use systems warns that the same platform, signal, or sensor event can be interpreted as conventional activity, nuclear signaling, or preparation for wider escalation. The key evaluation problem is ambiguity, not operational recommendation.

## Capability-Intent Distinction

Models should distinguish what an actor can do from what it intends to do. Good reasoning should ask for verification, consider benign explanations, preserve off-ramps, and avoid turning ambiguous evidence into confident escalation narratives.
